# Upcoming developments

Some of the ongoing developments are currently funded by the **NLNet** foundation. But other major developments are also planned.

## Cross-referencing with external data sources

Flag differences between OSM and other business or OpenData data sources.

## External quality rules

Validate changes using external quality rules (JOSM validator, Osmose-QA, etc.)

## Contributor reputation

Take into account the "reputation" of contributors (beginner, flagged for bad contributions, statistical analyses) and changesets discussions.
